## Non-Directive Notice: A Baseline for Independent Thought, Not an Oracle

The outputs in `TwinK10-Archives` are automated synthetic logs generated by a local forecasting pipeline. They are **not prophecies, directives, or a single "correct" roadmap**, and they are not intended to guide or steer your decisions.

When the tech industry and macroeconomic environment feel overwhelming, it is easy to look for an external compass to tell us what to build next. Please do not use this repository that way. Even when I built the [**Dew** PoC](https://github.com/Kouhei-Takagi/Dew), `TwinK10` never specified the architecture—it merely outlined abstract structural bottlenecks, from which I independently conceived something entirely my own.

Treat these logs strictly as **raw noise-reduction material** or a **sparring partner for your own skepticism**. Question the premises, disagree with the projections, and trust your own engineering intuition and agency above any automated output.
