- **Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP) & Off-Earth In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU):** Orbital solar collection arrays designed for wireless energy transmission to terrestrial grids, alongside extraterrestrial mineral harvesting (e.g., asteroid and lunar extraction). By sourcing primary energy and critical raw materials beyond the biosphere, these systems address resource exhaustion while preserving Earth's ecological stability.

### ⚡ Computation & AI Layer

These platforms drastically reduce thermal dissipation and power consumption per FLOP, scaling machine intelligence capabilities without breaching terrestrial energy and thermodynamic thresholds.
