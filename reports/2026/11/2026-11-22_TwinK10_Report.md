## ⚠️ Physical & Energy Limits
The current trajectory of artificial intelligence scaling faces hard thermodynamic and geopolitical boundaries. The structural vulnerability of centralized advanced semiconductor fabrication—predominantly concentrated in Taiwan—combined with state-level protectionism and resource hoarding of critical raw materials (such as copper and uranium) creates an impending, catastrophic shortfall in computational capacity and electrical grid distribution. Without architectural shifts, unregulated competition for energy inputs will accelerate environmental destruction and undermine the core infrastructural stability of human civilization.

Consequently, global investment vectors are migrating away from purely virtual, software-layer abstractions and concentrating heavily in capital-intensive physical infrastructure: critical resource security, advanced mineral extraction, and next-generation power generation facilities.

The 2032–2035 projection represents the backcasted minimum duration required to design, manufacture, and scale these replacement physical compute substrates to operational equilibrium.
